Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Can I put my café or hotel name on the bottle?

That is the core custom-branding concept. The final label must be produced and applied through an arrangement that meets applicable food-safety and labelling rules, with the required manufacturer and brand-owner declarations retained.

Can I order EQUIS-branded bottles instead?

Yes, the concept supports both EQUIS-branded designs and customer-specific branding. Actual supply is subject to manufacturer approval and compliance checks.

Can I buy sealed bottles and add my own sticker afterwards?

Do not assume this is permitted. Adding a label can make you a relabeller or otherwise affect mandatory declarations. Get written guidance from the relevant Food Safety authority and the licensed manufacturer before doing this.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities depend on the manufacturing partner, bottle format, label method and production run. Submit an enquiry to discuss a quote; no minimum is promised on this demo site.

Do you deliver outside Srinagar?

Delivery areas, lead times and transport costs will need to be confirmed for each order. You can mention your location in the enquiry.
